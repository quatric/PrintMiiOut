About

PrintMiiOut fetches your Mii, builds a head and body right in your browser, and hands you an STL ready to slice for your 3D printer. Print your Mii with supports turned on.

You can bring a Mii from a Nintendo Network ID (Wii U), a Pretendo Network ID, a binary Mii file (.mii or .ffsd), or a Mii QR code image. Glasses print as a flat frame with temple arms, and the figure can print as snap-together parts.

It also makes print-in-place bobbleheads, flat articulated Miis whose arms and legs swing on joints printed in place, coins with the face drawn flat (round, square or cut to the shape of the face), keychains like the ones Wii no Ma made, and round or square stamps to use with an ink pad, lettered with their name or your own text.

And for your regular printer: sheets of name stickers like Wii no Ma's, on sticker paper or Avery label sheets, plus towel and shirt designs for iron-on transfers.

Printer, filament and patience not included.
